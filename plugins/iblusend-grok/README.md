# iBluSend for Grok Build

Safely triage conversations, inspect contacts and devices, and send confirmed iMessages and SMS through one consented workspace.

This is the Grok Build copy of the `iblusend` plug-in. It carries the same skills and
the same MCP server as the Claude Code and Codex package in `plugins/iblusend`. Only the
manifest (`.grok-plugin/plugin.json`) and the sign-in settings in `.mcp.json` differ.

## Sign-in

The first time a tool runs, Grok opens iBluSend's Connect screen in your browser.
Pick one workspace and the areas you want your AI to use. Nothing is ticked for you. You can
reconnect later to add an area.

## Safety

Nothing sends, launches, books or charges without you. A text or a voice memo needs your yes in
chat first. A campaign launches only when you press Launch in iBluSend, and an
invoice goes out only when you press Send there. If you tick Calendar booking, a booking, move or
cancel happens only after you say yes to its preview; otherwise you press Schedule or Save in the
app. Contact edits, iMessage extras and booking reach only the contacts you approve under Manage
actions.
